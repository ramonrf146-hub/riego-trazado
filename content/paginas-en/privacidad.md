_Last updated: August 24, 2026._

This Privacy Policy describes how **HidroLab** ("we," "us," or "the site") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit riegocom.uk (the "Site").

## 1. Information we collect

- **Automatically collected usage information:** IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and similar data collected through cookies and analytics tools (see Section 3).
- **Information you provide voluntarily:** your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter, or any data you include when contacting us.

We do not collect financial or payment information — all purchases are processed directly on Amazon.com under its own policies.

## 2. How we use the information

- To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
- To send the newsletter to those who voluntarily subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time).
- To analyze aggregated usage patterns to understand what content is useful.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Cookies and analytics

This Site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand how content is used (pages visited, time on site, traffic source). Google Analytics uses its own and third-party cookies; you can opt out by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by rejecting cookies in your browser settings. Some Site features may be affected if you disable cookies.

## 4. Amazon Associates Program

**HidroLab is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com (and, where applicable, to Amazon.com.mx, Amazon.es, or other affiliated Amazon domains).

When you click a product link on this Site and make a purchase on Amazon, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Amazon may place its own cookies on your browser when you click these links; Amazon's use of that information is governed by [Amazon's Privacy Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496).

## 5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, non-identifiable data with analytics providers and with Amazon (through its own affiliate cookies) solely for the purposes described in this policy.

## 6. Links to third-party sites

This Site contains links to Amazon.com and, occasionally, to other external sites referenced in our guides. We do not control the content or privacy practices of those sites; we recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site before providing it with personal information.

## 7. Data retention

We retain newsletter subscription information until the user unsubscribes. Analytics data is retained according to the default retention settings of the tool used.

## 8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (for example, your newsletter email address) by writing to us through the [About](/en/acerca-de) page. If you reside in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or California, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or the CCPA (access, portability, deletion, and objection to processing); we honor them the same way for all users who request them, regardless of your location.

## 9. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version.
